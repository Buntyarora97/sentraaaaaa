What Counts as an Eye Emergency?

Use the emergency contacts above only for symptoms like these — situations where a delay of even a few hours could affect the final outcome for your vision.

⚡

Sudden Flashes or a Shower of Floaters

A sudden new burst of flashes or floaters, or a dark curtain/shadow spreading across your vision.

📉

Sudden Vision Loss

Any sudden drop or blackout in vision, in one or both eyes, with or without pain.

🤕

Serious Eye Injury

A direct blow, chemical splash, penetrating injury, or anything lodged in the eye.

For symptoms outside these categories, or if you're unsure, calling Ms. Anjali first is always the right move — she will advise you honestly on whether it needs immediate attention.
