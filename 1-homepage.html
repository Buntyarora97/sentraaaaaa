LASIK & Refractive Surgery at a Glance

If you have been dependent on spectacles or contact lenses for years, you will know the daily frustration — foggy glasses in the rain, uncomfortable lenses after a long workday, the anxiety of losing them during sport or travel. LASIK eye surgery in Malad, Mumbai at Sentra Clinic offers a safe, proven route to clear, natural vision — often achieved in a single 15-minute procedure.

We offer the full spectrum of refractive surgery, tailored to your corneal anatomy and lifestyle:

  • Standard Blade-Free LASIK — the gold standard for most patients with myopia, hyperopia or astigmatism
  • Contoura Vision (Topography-Guided LASIK) — maps 22,000 unique points on your cornea for sharper, more customised correction than standard LASIK
  • ICL (Implantable Collamer Lens) — the ideal solution for patients with thin corneas or very high prescriptions, where LASIK is not appropriate
  • PRK / LASEK — surface ablation when corneal thickness or a pre-existing condition rules out flap-based LASIK

Every refractive candidate undergoes a thorough pre-op screening including corneal topography, wavefront analysis, pachymetry and a dilated examination — because the right procedure chosen at the right time is what delivers life-changing results. Cataract Surgery at a Glance

Cataracts — the gradual clouding of the eye's natural lens — are the world's leading cause of reversible blindness, and they affect millions of people across Mumbai alone. When a cataract begins to blur your vision, make colours appear washed out, or cause glare and halos around lights at night, surgery is the only effective treatment. The good news: cataract surgery in Malad at Sentra Clinic is one of the safest, most refined procedures in all of medicine.

Dr. Rohit Modi has performed over 10,000 cataract and retinal surgeries over 13+ years. He uses micro-incision phacoemulsification — a technique so gentle that sutures are rarely needed and most patients are back to their daily routine within a day or two. Equally important is the choice of intraocular lens (IOL), which Dr. Modi and Dr. Surekha discuss with every patient individually:

  • Monofocal IOL — reliable distance vision; spectacles may be needed for near tasks
  • Toric IOL — corrects pre-existing astigmatism at the same time as removing the cataract
  • Multifocal / EDOF IOL — premium lenses designed to give you a full range of vision, reducing dependence on spectacles at all distances

If you or a family member has been told it is "time to consider cataract surgery", or if you would like to understand your cataract surgery cost in Mumbai, our team is happy to walk you through every option at no obligation.

Retina & Diabetic Eye Care at a Glance

The retina is the thin layer of light-sensitive tissue at the back of the eye — and it is where your brain actually "sees". Diseases of the retina, if caught early, are treatable. Left undetected, they can cause permanent, irreversible vision loss. This is why access to a skilled retina specialist in Malad, Mumbai is so critical — and why Dr. Rohit Modi built Sentra Clinic with a particular focus on retinal disease.

The most common retinal threat in urban Mumbai is diabetic retinopathy — damage to the blood vessels of the retina caused by uncontrolled blood sugar. If you have had diabetes for 5 or more years, you should be having an annual dilated retina examination. Many patients are shocked to learn they already have early retinopathy with no symptoms at all. Catching it at this stage allows simple laser or injection treatment that preserves vision for decades.

We also diagnose and manage diabetic retinopathy, age-related macular degeneration (ARMD), retinal vein occlusions, retinal tears and detachment, and vitreous floaters. Treatment options include:

  • Intravitreal anti-VEGF injections (Lucentis, Avastin, Eylea)
  • Laser photocoagulation and micropulse laser
  • Vitreoretinal surgery for complex cases

Cornea Care at a Glance

The cornea — the clear, dome-shaped front surface of the eye — determines the quality of everything you see. When disease, injury or a hereditary condition like keratoconus weakens or clouds the cornea, vision deteriorates in ways that spectacles simply cannot correct. Dr. Shraddha Surekha, who completed advanced cornea training at LV Prasad Eye Institute, leads our cornea service at Sentra Clinic.

Our cornea services include:

  • Keratoconus Management — including C3R corneal cross-linking to halt progressive thinning and bulging, and customised contact lens fitting for irregular corneas
  • Corneal Transplantation — DALK (Deep Anterior Lamellar Keratoplasty) and DSAEK (Descemet's Stripping Automated Endothelial Keratoplasty) for advanced corneal disease
  • Pterygium Surgery — glue-free, suture-free technique for the wing-shaped growth that encroaches on the cornea, with minimal recurrence risk
  • Corneal Infections — urgent diagnosis and treatment of bacterial, fungal and viral keratitis
  • Dry Eye & Ocular Surface Disorders — from mild aqueous-deficient dry eye to complex mucous-membrane pemphigoid, with a stepwise treatment approach

Dr. Surekha also contributed to research on limbal epithelial transplantation — a technique for restoring the corneal surface in patients with limbal stem cell deficiency — underscoring Sentra Clinic's commitment to evidence-based, cutting-edge care even in a suburban Mumbai setting.

  2. Registration & Intake
    Our front-desk team will take your basic details, ask about any existing spectacle prescription, medications and general health conditions (diabetes, hypertension, prior eye surgery). Please bring your current spectacles and any old prescriptions you have.
  3. Preliminary Tests by Our Technicians
    A trained ophthalmic technician will perform a computerised refraction (vision test), intraocular pressure check (glaucoma screening), and colour vision test. If you are visiting for a retina or cataract concern, additional tests such as OCT or fundus photography may be done at this stage.
  4. Doctor Consultation
    You will be seen personally by Dr. Rohit Modi or Dr. Shraddha Surekha — never delegated to a junior. The doctor will review your test results, perform a detailed slit-lamp examination, and carry out a dilated fundus examination if clinically indicated. Note: if dilation is planned, please bring a companion or arrange transport, as your near vision will be blurred for 3–4 hours.
  5. Diagnosis & Discussion
    Your doctor will explain what has been found — clearly, in language you can understand, using images from the diagnostic equipment where helpful. If surgery or a procedure is recommended, the doctor explains why it is needed, what it involves, what the alternatives are, and what happens if it is deferred.
  6. Treatment Plan & Cost Estimate
    If a procedure is planned, our team provides a written cost estimate before you commit to anything. We check your insurance eligibility at this stage and advise on any cashless or EMI options that apply to your treatment.
  7. Follow-Up Scheduling
    Whether you are coming back for treatment or just for a routine review, your next appointment is booked before you leave the clinic. We send reminders via WhatsApp so nothing slips through the cracks.
Tip for LASIK candidates: If you wear soft contact lenses, remove them 3 days before your consultation. Hard / RGP lens wearers should remove them for 3 weeks. This allows your corneal curvature to stabilise before topography measurements are taken — giving the most accurate candidacy assessment.

Am I a good candidate for LASIK surgery?

LASIK suitability depends on your corneal thickness, curvature, prescription stability, and general ocular health — factors that can only be assessed after a thorough pre-operative evaluation including corneal topography and pachymetry. Most people between 18 and 40 with stable myopia, hyperopia or astigmatism are candidates. If your corneas are thin or your prescription is very high, ICL lens implantation may be a better alternative. What is the difference between LASIK, Contoura Vision, and ICL?

All three correct refractive errors (myopia, astigmatism, hyperopia), but they work differently. Standard LASIK uses a laser to reshape the corneal surface. Contoura Vision is a topography-guided variant of LASIK that maps 22,000 points on your cornea for a more precise, personalised correction — often delivering sharper vision than standard LASIK. ICL (Implantable Collamer Lens) involves placing a lens inside the eye rather than reshaping the cornea — the preferred option for high prescriptions or thin corneas that do not qualify for laser treatment. The right choice depends on your corneal measurements, prescription and lifestyle, and can only be determined after a proper examination.
