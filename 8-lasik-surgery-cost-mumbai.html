What Decides the Price?

🔬

Technology Used

Topography-guided (Contoura) and lens-based (ICL) cost more than standard LASIK. The more sophisticated the mapping and the laser platform, the higher the investment — and the more personalised your result.

👁️

Your Eye Profile

Power, cornea thickness and dryness can rule technologies in or out. Your scan determines which procedure is safe and effective for your eyes — not your budget.

📋

What's Included

Quality clinics include pre-op scans, both-eye surgery, medications and follow-ups. Cheap quotes often exclude these — ask. A ₹10,000 headline price that excludes scans is not a bargain.

🧑‍⚕️

Surgeon Experience

You're paying for judgement, not just laser time. A fellowship-trained refractive surgeon brings diagnostic precision and the ability to manage complications that less experienced providers cannot.

LASIK vs Contoura vs ICL — Cost vs Value

✅

Standard LASIK

Delivers excellent results for routine powers at the lowest price. The proven workhorse of refractive surgery — ideal for straightforward prescriptions in good corneal candidates.

🗺️

Contoura Vision

Adds personalised corneal mapping — worth it for night drivers and precision-demanding professions. The topography-guided approach corrects both power and corneal irregularities simultaneously.

💎

ICL (Implantable Collamer Lens)

Costs the most because the lens is custom-made for your eye — and for high powers, it's not a luxury but the only option. Your scan tells you which conversation to have.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum cost of LASIK in Mumbai?

Entry-level LASIK packages in Mumbai start around the lower end of the standard range, but verify what's included — scans, both eyes, medicines and follow-ups. A complete transparent package protects you from counter-surprises.

Is LASIK cost covered by insurance?

Usually not, as LASIK is considered elective — though some corporate policies and high-power cases (typically above a threshold) may qualify. We'll help you check your specific policy.

Why is ICL so much costlier than LASIK?

The ICL lens is individually manufactured to your eye's measurements and imported — the implant itself is the major cost. For very high powers, it's the only effective specs-removal option.
