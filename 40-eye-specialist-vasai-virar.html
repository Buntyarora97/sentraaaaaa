Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the journey from Virar to Sentra Clinic?

By Western Railway fast train, Virar to Malad is approximately 35–42 minutes. Add 10 minutes by auto-rickshaw from Malad Station to Shah Arcade 2, Rani Sati Road. Total door-to-door: approximately 50–55 minutes from Virar.

Is there a good eye hospital in Vasai-Virar itself?

Vasai-Virar has general hospitals and optometrists, but no dedicated eye hospital with in-house OT, fellowship-trained surgeons, and the full range of surgical services. Sentra Clinic in Malad East is the nearest comprehensive surgical eye facility for the entire Vasai-Virar-Nalasopara belt.

How many visits are needed for LASIK from Vasai-Virar?

Typically two visits: (1) Pre-operative assessment — corneal topography, refraction, dilated eye exam. This takes 2–3 hours due to dilating drops. (2) LASIK surgery — 1.5–2 hours at the clinic. All follow-ups can be discussed for scheduling convenience, with WhatsApp check-ins where clinically appropriate.

Can I travel home by train after surgery on the same day?

Yes — for LASIK and cataract surgery, most patients travel home by train on the day of surgery, typically 3–4 hours after the procedure, with a companion. We advise sunglasses for light sensitivity. We will confirm specific travel guidance based on your procedure.
