Approximately 15–20 minutes by road from Jogeshwari East or West. By Western Railway train, Jogeshwari to Malad is just 3 stops (about 10 minutes on a fast train), then 10 minutes by auto-rickshaw to Rani Sati Road.

Is there an eye hospital in Jogeshwari itself?

Jogeshwari has optometrists and general hospitals with eye OPDs. However, for surgical eye care — LASIK eligibility testing, cataract evaluation with biometry, retinal examination or glaucoma management — Sentra Clinic in Malad East is the nearest dedicated surgical eye hospital for Jogeshwari residents.

Can I bring my child for an eye examination?

Yes — Sentra Clinic offers paediatric eye examinations for children from 1 year of age. We check for refractive errors, amblyopia (lazy eye), squint, and colour blindness. A cycloplegic refraction (eye drops to check the true power) takes about 1.5 hours including the drops' effect, so plan accordingly.
