Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Sentra Clinic from Santacruz?

By Western Railway fast train, Santacruz to Malad is approximately 20–25 minutes (7 stops). Add 10 minutes by auto to Rani Sati Road. By road via WEH, approximately 25–30 minutes by cab.

Why come to Malad when there are eye clinics in Santacruz?

Santacruz and nearby Bandra have optometrists and general hospital eye OPDs, but for dedicated surgical eye care — LASIK with corneal topography assessment, cataract surgery with biometry and premium IOL selection, retinal surgery — Sentra Clinic in Malad offers the full range under one roof with resident fellowship surgeons.

Is parking available at Sentra Clinic?

Limited parking is available in the Shah Arcade 2 compound. We recommend coming by Western Railway train + auto for the most hassle-free journey, especially for consultation visits that involve pupil dilation (you cannot drive after dilation).
