LASIK Options Available — What Works Best for Goregaon West Patients

✨

Contoura Vision (Our Most Recommended)

Topography-guided LASIK uses a personalised map of your cornea's 22,000 elevation points to guide the excimer laser. Corrects the unique optical imperfections in your cornea that standard glasses and LASIK cannot address. Most Goregaon West patients who commute, work on screens, or drive at night prefer Contoura Vision for its superior contrast and reduced halo/glare outcomes.

💫

SMILE Surgery (Flapless)

SMILE removes a tiny lenticule from inside the cornea through a 4mm incision — no flap. Preferred for active lifestyles (cricket, football, gym) and patients with mild pre-existing dry eye. Goregaon West's active young population is an ideal demographic for SMILE. Recovery is slightly slower than LASIK for the first few days, but long-term outcomes are equivalent.

💠

ICL Surgery (For High Power / Thin Corneas)

When corneas are too thin or prescription is too high for LASIK, EVO Visian ICL (Implantable Collamer Lens) is the gold-standard alternative. A lens is placed inside the eye between the iris and natural lens — fully reversible, no corneal tissue removed. Particularly popular among Goregaon West professionals with myopia above −8 to −10 diopters who want excellent visual quality.

Can I get LASIK if my power changes every year?

LASIK requires a stable prescription for at least 1–2 years. If your power is still changing, LASIK should wait — operating on an unstable prescription increases regression risk. However, if you're in a myopia progression phase, we can discuss myopia control options to stabilise your power first.

What if I am not a LASIK candidate after the workup?

Not everyone is a LASIK candidate — and we won't recommend LASIK if it is not safe for you. The most common alternatives are SMILE surgery (for borderline dry eye) and ICL surgery (for thin corneas or very high myopia). Both achieve excellent visual outcomes. Dr. Shraddha will explain your options clearly and without pressure at the consultation.

Is Contoura Vision significantly better than standard LASIK?

Yes — in peer-reviewed clinical studies, Contoura Vision shows higher rates of patients achieving 6/6 or better compared to standard LASIK, and significantly fewer patients report post-operative halos and glare at night.
