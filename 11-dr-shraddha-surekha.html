Her Approach: Diagnose First, Operate Only When Necessary

Corneal conditions are often misdiagnosed or overtreated. Dr. Shraddha Sureka's training at LV Prasad — India's reference centre for complex corneal disease — means she has seen the full range of presentations, from early keratoconus picked up on a topography scan to sight-threatening corneal ulcers requiring urgent grafting.

Her approach is methodical: map the cornea, understand the tear film, rule out systemic causes, and then choose the least invasive intervention that achieves the result. Patients often find that a condition they were told "requires surgery immediately" can be managed medically — and she will tell you so, clearly, in Hindi, Marathi or English.

Frequently Asked Questions

What conditions does Dr. Shraddha Sureka treat?

Dr. Sureka specialises in laser vision correction (LASIK, SMILE, PRK), advanced cataract surgery with premium lenses, corneal transplantation (lamellar and full-thickness), keratoconus management including corneal cross-linking, and dry eye and ocular surface disorders.

Is she qualified to perform corneal transplants?

Yes. Dr. Sureka completed her cornea fellowship at LV Prasad Eye Institute — India's premier centre for corneal surgery — and has performed both lamellar (DSAEK, DALK) and penetrating keratoplasty procedures.

Can she assess whether I am suitable for LASIK?

Absolutely. Refractive surgery candidacy assessment — including corneal topography, pachymetry (thickness measurement) and tear-film evaluation — is central to her practice. She will tell you honestly if you are a good candidate, a borderline candidate, or if an alternative like ICL would suit you better.

I have been told I have keratoconus. What can she do?

Keratoconus management starts with accurate staging. Early disease may be monitored or halted with corneal collagen cross-linking (CXL). Moderate disease may benefit from Intacs rings. Advanced disease causing significant vision loss may require a corneal transplant. Dr. Sureka will stage your condition and explain the options at each stage.
