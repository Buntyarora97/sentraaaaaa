Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Sentra Clinic from Thane?

By road, approximately 45–55 minutes via Eastern Express Highway south → JVLR → WEH north → Malad East exit. Cab fare approximately ₹320–450 one way from Thane West.

Are there no eye hospitals closer to Thane?

Thane has general hospitals with eye departments and several optometrists. For dedicated surgical care with fellowship-trained surgeons, advanced diagnostics and in-house OT, Sentra Clinic in Malad East is the nearest facility matching those credentials in the western corridor. Many Thane patients also compare it favourably with South Mumbai hospitals that are significantly farther and more expensive.

How many visits are needed from Thane for LASIK?

Typically 2–3 visits: (1) Pre-op assessment (corneal topography, refraction, dilated exam — plan 3 hours as dilation affects vision). (2) LASIK surgery day. (3) Day-1 post-op check (mandatory). Subsequent follow-ups at 1 week, 1 month and 3 months — some can be managed via WhatsApp check-in for Thane patients depending on the surgeon's assessment.
