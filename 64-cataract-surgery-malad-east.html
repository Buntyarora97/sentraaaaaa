Signs Your Parent or Family Member Needs a Cataract Assessment

These are the most common symptoms that bring Malad East families to Sentra Clinic for a cataract evaluation:

  • Blurred, cloudy, or foggy vision that glasses no longer fully correct
  • Glare and halos around streetlights and car headlights — making night driving difficult or impossible
  • Colours appearing faded or yellowish — white seems cream-coloured, blues look washed out
  • Prescription changing frequently — needing new glasses every 6–8 months
  • Double vision in one eye (not corrected by glasses)
  • Difficulty reading even with reading glasses in good lighting
  • "Second sight" — patient suddenly reads better without glasses (a classic nuclear cataract sign)
⚠️ If you recognise these symptoms in a parent or family member in Malad East, book a cataract assessment at Sentra Clinic. The evaluation takes 60–90 minutes and gives you a clear picture of the cataract grade and the appropriate timing for surgery.

Cataract Surgery — Step by Step for Malad East Patients

  1. Cataract Assessment at Sentra Clinic — Slit lamp cataract grading, visual acuity testing, IOL power calculation (optical biometry with IOL Master), intraocular pressure, and dilated retina exam (to rule out concurrent retinal disease that could limit surgical benefit).
  2. IOL Counselling — Dr. Modi explains lens options: standard monofocal, EDOF, or premium trifocal. For Malad East seniors who want to read WhatsApp messages, use a smartphone, and watch TV all without glasses — the trifocal IOL is typically the most life-changing choice.
  3. Pre-operative Blood Work — CBC, blood sugar (HbA1c for diabetics), BP, ECG arranged through your physician. Antibiotic drops started 2 days before surgery.
  4. Surgery Day — Arrive 30–45 min early. Dilating drops instilled. 15–20 minute surgery under topical anaesthesia (eye drops — no injections, no general anaesthesia). An eye shield placed post-surgery. Home within 2 hours.
  5. Day 1 Follow-Up — Vision check at Sentra Clinic the morning after surgery. Most Malad East patients walk in without help — they can already see clearly enough to read the eye chart. Eye shield removed, drops prescription given.
  6. Week 1, Month 1, Month 3 Follow-Up — All at Sentra Clinic, Malad East. The convenience of staying local for all follow-up is a key reason Malad East families consistently prefer Sentra Clinic.

Does cataract surgery require hospital admission overnight?

No. Modern phacoemulsification cataract surgery is a day procedure. Most Malad East patients arrive at the surgical facility in the morning and are home the same afternoon. No overnight stay required.

What happens if I have diabetes and need cataract surgery?

Diabetes is very common among Malad East's senior population and does not prevent cataract surgery. We require that blood sugar is reasonably controlled pre-operatively (fasting BSL ideally below 200 mg/dL) and use intensive antibiotic drops to reduce the slightly elevated infection risk. We co-ordinate with your physician for pre-operative optimisation.

Will my parent need glasses after trifocal IOL surgery?

Most patients with trifocal IOLs achieve spectacle independence — they can read, use a phone, watch TV, and drive without glasses. A small percentage may need thin reading glasses for very small print or prolonged close work. Overall, the chance of needing glasses for any task is very low with premium trifocal lenses.

How soon after surgery can my parent resume normal activities?

Normal daily activities — watching TV, light reading, walking, cooking — can resume within 2–3 days. Activities to avoid for 4 weeks: swimming, heavy lifting, eye-rubbing, and dusty environments. Final glasses prescription (if any) is given at 4–6 weeks when the eye has fully stabilised.
