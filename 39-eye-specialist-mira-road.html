Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Sentra Clinic from Mira Road?

Approximately 30–35 minutes by road via the Western Express Highway, or 30–35 minutes by Western Railway fast train (Mira Road to Malad: ~18–22 min) plus 10 min auto to Rani Sati Road.

Is Mira Road in Mumbai or Thane?

Mira-Bhayandar is in Thane district, though it's part of the greater Mumbai metropolitan area. The nearest dedicated surgical eye hospital for Mira Road residents is Sentra Clinic in Malad East, Mumbai — significantly closer than Thane city or South Mumbai hospitals.

Can follow-up visits after surgery be done closer to Mira Road?

For the first post-op day and one-week check, visiting Sentra is essential for accurate assessment by the operating surgeon. Routine 3-month and 6-month follow-ups can sometimes be managed with a WhatsApp video check-in plus a local refraction, subject to the surgeon's advice for your specific case.

What is the cab fare from Mira Road to Sentra Clinic?

Typically ₹220–320 one-way by Ola/Uber from Mira Road or Bhayandar to Malad East, depending on traffic and surge pricing. Western Railway train + auto is more economical at approximately ₹80 total each way.
