The Core Difference: Power vs Fingerprint

Every prescription — say, -4.5 with -1.0 cylinder — describes your eye's focusing error in two numbers. Standard LASIK programs the laser with those numbers: accurate, proven, effective for the vast majority of patients.

But corneas also carry unique micro-irregularities that two prescription numbers can't capture — subtle elevations and valleys that scatter light, causing glare and that "sharp but not SHARP" feeling even with perfect glasses. These are called higher-order aberrations, and they vary from cornea to cornea like fingerprints.

Contoura's topographer maps these irregularities at thousands of measurement points and the laser smooths them during the same treatment. Same procedure experience, same recovery time — a different level of optical finishing. The analogy: standard LASIK is like printing a photo in high resolution; Contoura is like printing it in ultra-high resolution with colour calibration for your specific monitor.

Results: What "Sharper" Actually Means

In Contoura's US FDA clinical trial, a substantial proportion of treated eyes achieved vision measurably better than 6/6 — and many patients reported seeing better than they ever did even with their best glasses. This is the "sharper than glasses" claim that gets used in Contoura marketing, and it is clinically real — for eyes with significant higher-order aberrations that the topography-guided treatment corrects.

Translate this honestly for your own eyes: both procedures aim for spectacle freedom; Contoura additionally polishes the optical quality of that vision — contrast sensitivity, night definition, reduced starbursts around lights. Patients who notice the difference most tend to be:

  • Night drivers who currently see halos or starbursts around headlights
  • Screen-heavy professionals (designers, architects, surgeons) who need fine detail resolution
  • Patients whose topography shows measurable irregularity — the technology exists for exactly their cornea
  • Anyone who has tried multiple glasses prescriptions without ever feeling "100% clear"

For patients with smooth corneas and routine prescriptions, the marginal gain of Contoura over standard LASIK may be smaller — excellent standard LASIK is a genuinely excellent outcome, not a compromise.

The Technology Behind Contoura

Understanding the technology makes the price more legible. Contoura (also called topography-guided LASIK) uses the VIVITY or Wavelight/Alcon platform's corneal topographer to capture a detailed elevation map of the anterior corneal surface — far more data than a wavefront or standard refraction measurement captures.

This map is then merged with the refractive prescription and fed to the excimer laser as a customised ablation profile. The laser doesn't just remove tissue for the prescription — it sculpts a smoother, more optically ideal surface that accounts for the cornea's individual peaks and valleys. The result, for suitable eyes, is a cornea that performs better optically after surgery than it did with its natural surface.

The 22,000-point figure comes from the topographer's measurement density — a clinically meaningful level of detail that wavefront alone doesn't match at the corneal surface where laser ablation operates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Contoura better than LASIK for everyone?

No — its edge is real but conditional. Eyes with corneal irregularity or night-vision-critical lifestyles benefit most; smooth-cornea routine cases do excellently with standard LASIK. The topography scan identifies which group your eyes fall into.

Contoura aur LASIK mein price ka kitna fark hai?

Contoura premium category mein aata hai — exact difference technology package par depend karta hai. Scan ke baad likhit all-inclusive quote milta hai; hamari cost guide par realistic ranges diye hain. LASIK aur Contoura cost guide dekhein →

Is Contoura recovery longer than LASIK?

No — same-day procedure, same drops, same 1–2 day return to desk work. The difference is in optical finishing quality, not recovery timeline. Patients from both options return to screens by day 2–3 and outdoor activity by week 2.

Can I upgrade to Contoura on surgery day?

The decision should be made at the scan stage, since Contoura planning uses your topography capture from the pre-op evaluation. Trying to upgrade on the day of surgery is not ideal practice. Booking the scan first keeps every option open and lets the recommendation be based on actual corneal data.

Kya Contoura se chasma 100% jayega?

Eligible candidates mein spectacle freedom ki rate dono procedures (LASIK aur Contoura) mein bahut high hai. Contoura additionally optical quality improve karta hai — yani sirf number nahi jaata, balki vision ki clarity bhi behtar ho sakti hai. Exact outcome pre-op scan ke baad discuss hota hai.

SMILE vs Contoura — which is better?

Different technologies for different needs. SMILE is flapless (appeals to contact-sport lifestyles, typically milder dry-eye impact); Contoura is topography-guided (best for corneal irregularity and optical quality). See our full comparison: SMILE vs LASIK vs Contoura →
