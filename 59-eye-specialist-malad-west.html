Eye Health Issues Prevalent in Malad West

Malad West's geographic and demographic character creates some specific eye health concerns that residents should be aware of:

🌬️

Coastal Living & Eye Health — Dry Eye, Allergies

Living close to the coast (Marve, Malvani) exposes residents to sea breeze, higher humidity, and wind-borne salt particles. While humidity can reduce dry eye severity, the combination of sea air, pollution, and screen exposure creates a complex tear film environment. Many Malad West residents near the coast present with allergic conjunctivitis (itchy, red, watery eyes seasonally) and meibomian gland dysfunction. Our dry eye clinic evaluates the specific cause and prescribes targeted treatment.

💎

Cataract Surgery — Premium Lens Options

Among the Malad West senior population, cataract is the single most common treatable cause of vision loss. Many seniors are under the impression that cataract surgery requires a long recovery, large incisions, or a hospital stay. Modern phacoemulsification cataract surgery uses a 2.2mm incision, takes 15–20 minutes, requires no sutures, and most patients return home the same day. Premium trifocal IOL lenses allow clear vision at near, intermediate, and distance — many patients do not need any glasses after surgery.

✨

LASIK & Specs Removal for Young Adults

Young Malad West adults — particularly those commuting to offices in Andheri, BKC, or working in local businesses — have strong demand for LASIK and Contoura Vision specs removal. The convenience of waking up with clear vision, not worrying about glasses during the monsoon, and being able to pursue sports and outdoor activities without vision aids is a powerful motivator. Full pre-LASIK screening is done in one visit at Sentra Clinic.

🩺

Diabetic Eye Checks

With a significant portion of the Malad West adult population living with Type 2 diabetes, regular dilated retina examinations are critical. Diabetic retinopathy — the leading cause of new blindness in working-age adults in India — causes no symptoms until it is advanced. An annual 15-minute retina exam at Sentra Clinic can prevent the progression from early, treatable retinopathy to sight-threatening complications.

👁️

Squint & Children's Eye Health

Squint (strabismus) and amblyopia (lazy eye) in children are time-sensitive conditions — the window for optimal treatment is before age 7–8, when the visual cortex is still plastic enough to respond to therapy. Many Malad West children are brought to us after being told by school medicals that "there's a problem" but without specific diagnosis or management. We provide complete paediatric ophthalmology assessment and treatment.

🔵

Glaucoma Screening for Middle-Aged Adults

Any Malad West resident over 40 who has never had a tonometry (eye pressure) check is potentially at risk for undiagnosed glaucoma. This silent condition irreversibly damages the optic nerve over time. Our annual glaucoma screening (IOP measurement + OCT nerve fibre analysis) takes under 20 minutes and is the most cost-effective investment in your long-term vision.

What does cataract surgery cost at Sentra Clinic?

The cost of cataract surgery depends on the type of IOL (lens) chosen — monofocal, trifocal, or multifocal. We offer clear, itemised pricing with no hidden charges. Please call 93729 47075 or WhatsApp for a consultation to get exact figures. EMI options are available through our banking partners.

Is LASIK permanent? Will my power come back?

LASIK reshapes your cornea permanently. In the vast majority of cases, the correction is permanent. A small percentage of patients may need a minor enhancement years later if natural lens changes occur, particularly after age 40. The success rate of LASIK at our partnered surgical centre is well above 98% for achieving 6/6 or better vision.

Do you treat eye allergies in Malad West patients?

Yes. Allergic conjunctivitis (itchy, red, watery eyes) is very common in Mumbai, especially in coastal areas. We diagnose the type of allergy, rule out infection, and prescribe targeted antihistamine and mast-cell stabiliser drops or systemic treatment as needed. Seasonal allergy management plans are also available.

At what age should I bring my child for a first eye exam?

We recommend a first comprehensive eye exam at age 3–4 to screen for lazy eye, squint, and refractive errors. If no problems are found, the next routine check is at age 5 (school entry). Thereafter, annual exams are recommended for all school-going children, especially if there is a family history of glasses or squint.
