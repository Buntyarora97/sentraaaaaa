Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Sentra Clinic from Dahisar?

Approximately 20–25 minutes south by road via the Western Express Highway. By Western Railway fast train, Dahisar to Malad is about 12–15 minutes (4 stops), then 10 minutes by auto to Rani Sati Road.

Is there a surgical eye hospital in Dahisar?

Dahisar has general hospitals with eye OPDs and some optometry clinics, but no dedicated eye hospital with in-house OT and resident fellowship-trained surgeons. Sentra Clinic in Malad East is the nearest full-service surgical eye hospital for Dahisar residents.

Do you also see patients from Mira Road?

Yes — patients from Mira Road, Mira-Bhayandar and Kashimira regularly visit Sentra Clinic. The journey via WEH is approximately 30–35 minutes from Mira Road to Malad East.

Can I get a diabetic retinal screening without a referral?

Absolutely. Walk-in or book directly by phone or WhatsApp. No referral is needed. We recommend annual retinal screenings for all diabetic patients, even those without vision symptoms.
