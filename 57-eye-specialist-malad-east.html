LASIK & Contoura Vision Surgery

Freedom from glasses with blade-free LASIK or topography-guided Contoura Vision. Ideal for Malad East adults between 18–40 with stable prescription who want permanent vision correction. Full pre-LASIK workup done on the first visit. Surgery same-day or next-day in most cases.

Modern micro-incision phacoemulsification takes 15–20 minutes and restores clear vision. Premium lens options (trifocal IOL) allow patients to see clearly at all distances — near, intermediate, and far — often without glasses after surgery. Affordable packages available.

Retina Specialist & Diabetic Eye Care

A 45-minute comprehensive eye assessment for the whole family — children aged 5+, working adults, and seniors. Includes vision test, glasses/lenses prescription, eye pressure measurement, corneal health, and dilated fundus examination.

Glaucoma Detection & Treatment

Glaucoma steals sight silently. Sentra Clinic's annual glaucoma screening — IOP measurement with Goldmann applanation, OCT optic nerve fibre analysis, visual field testing — ensures it is caught before irreversible damage occurs. Medical and surgical management available.

Children's Eye Specialist

School screening often misses subtle problems. Our paediatric eye examination covers lazy eye (amblyopia), squint, myopia progression, and colour vision. Early intervention before age 8 dramatically improves long-term visual outcomes for Malad East children.

Frequently Asked Questions — Eye Specialist in Malad East

Where exactly is Sentra Clinic in Malad East?

Sentra Clinic is at Shah Arcade 2, 002, First Floor, B Wing, Rani Sati Road, Malad East, Mumbai 400097. The nearest landmark is the Rani Sati Road Post Office. If you're coming by metro, Malad Station (Line 1) is the nearest stop — a 5-minute auto from there.

Can I walk in without an appointment?

Yes, walk-ins are welcome. However, for specific consultations — LASIK evaluation, pre-surgical assessments, or retina examinations requiring dilation — we recommend booking in advance so we can allocate sufficient time. Call 93729 47075 or message us on WhatsApp.

What is the consultation fee?

Please call 93729 47075 or WhatsApp us for current consultation and investigation charges. We keep our fees fair and transparent — all charges are communicated clearly before any investigation or procedure begins.

Do you treat children's eye problems?

Yes. We see children from age 5 onwards for myopia, squint (strabismus), lazy eye (amblyopia), colour vision testing, and routine eye health checks. Early detection in childhood is particularly important — many conditions respond far better to treatment when caught before age 7–8.

Is LASIK surgery done at Sentra Clinic itself or elsewhere?

The pre-LASIK evaluation (all screening tests) is done at Sentra Clinic. LASIK surgery itself is performed at our partnered laser vision centre, where we use the latest blade-free excimer and femtosecond laser platforms. Your surgeon from Sentra accompanies your case throughout — you do not hand off to a different doctor for the surgery.

How long does a comprehensive eye checkup take?

A full comprehensive eye examination including pre-tests, doctor consultation, and dilated fundus examination typically takes 60–90 minutes. Please plan for this time, especially if dilation is required (your near vision may be temporarily blurred for 3–4 hours after dilation).

Do you offer home visits for elderly patients who cannot travel?

For elderly or mobility-impaired patients in Malad East, please call us to discuss options. While our in-clinic equipment cannot be replicated at home, we can advise on initial assessment and arrange assisted transport if needed.

Are there EMI options for surgery?

Yes. EMI options are available for LASIK, cataract surgery, and other elective procedures through our banking partners. Details are discussed at the time of consultation — please ask our team when you visit.
