Frequently Asked Questions

What conditions does Dr. Rohit Modi treat?

Dr. Modi treats the full spectrum of eye conditions: refractive errors (LASIK, Contoura, ICL), retinal disease (diabetic retinopathy, retinal detachment, macular degeneration, anti-VEGF), cataract with premium IOLs, glaucoma, paediatric eye disease, squint, and general eye examinations for all age groups.

What are Dr. Modi's consultation timings?

Dr. Modi's OPD runs Monday to Saturday (Sundays closed). Monday: 11 am – 1 pm & 6 – 8:30 pm; Tuesday: 1 – 5 pm & 7:30 – 9 pm; Wednesday & Thursday: 4:30 – 8:30 pm; Friday: 3 – 8 pm; Saturday: 1:30 – 8:30 pm. Walk-ins are welcome; booking by phone or WhatsApp ensures a confirmed slot.

Does Dr. Modi perform the surgery himself?

Yes. At Sentra Clinic, Dr. Modi personally performs your pre-operative evaluation, the surgery, and your key follow-up appointments — care is not handed off to a junior doctor at any stage.

Can Dr. Modi assess whether I am suitable for LASIK?

Absolutely. Refractive surgery candidacy assessment — including corneal topography, pachymetry (thickness), pupil size measurement and tear-film evaluation — is central to his practice. He will tell you honestly whether LASIK, Contoura, ICL or no surgery at all is the right choice for your specific eyes.

I have a very high power (above –8). Can he help?

Yes. Patients with high myopia (–8 to –20 D and above) who are not LASIK candidates are often excellent ICL (Implantable Collamer Lens) candidates. Dr. Modi has extensive experience with ICL surgery for high myopia. The assessment determines eligibility and the best procedure for your specific power and corneal parameters.

Which languages does Dr. Modi consult in?

Hindi, Marathi and English — he explains diagnoses and procedures in the language you think and ask questions in, adapting based on what makes the patient most comfortable.
