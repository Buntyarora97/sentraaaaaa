Eye Conditions Extremely Common in Goregaon East

The demographics of Goregaon East — corporate professionals, content creators, students — make certain eye conditions particularly prevalent. Here is what our Goregaon East patients most commonly come in for:

💻

Digital Eye Strain (Computer Vision Syndrome)

Working 8–12 hours on screens without proper breaks causes blurred vision, eye fatigue, headaches, and dry eyes. This is not just tiredness — it is a clinical condition. We assess your blink rate, tear film, screen habits, and prescribe corrective measures including specialised lenses, lubricating drops, and workspace adjustments.

👓

Progressive Myopia (Increasing Spectacle Power)

Myopia — short-sightedness — is accelerating in young Goregaon East adults, driven by prolonged near-work and reduced outdoor time. We evaluate the rate of myopia progression and offer myopia control options, as well as LASIK or Contoura Vision for those 18+ with a stable prescription who want permanent freedom from glasses.

💧

Dry Eye Disease

Mumbai's pollution, air-conditioned offices, and screen time combine to dramatically reduce blink frequency and tear quality. Dry eye disease is often under-diagnosed — many patients use OTC drops for years without addressing the root cause. Our dry eye clinic performs meaurements of tear break-up time, Schirmer's test, and meibomian gland evaluation to prescribe targeted treatment.

🌟

Cataract (Especially in Parents & Grandparents)

If your parents or in-laws living in Goregaon East are experiencing cloudy vision, glare from headlights, or difficulty reading, early-stage cataract is a very common cause. Modern phacoemulsification (micro-incision cataract surgery) is done in 15 minutes and restores clear vision — often with the option to correct glasses power simultaneously via premium IOL lenses.

🩺

Diabetic Retinopathy

India has one of the highest rates of diabetes in the world, and Goregaon East's corporate lifestyle — sedentary jobs, irregular meals, high stress — is a known risk factor. Diabetic retinopathy causes no symptoms in its early stages, which is precisely why an annual dilated retina examination is essential for every diabetic. We use high-resolution fundus photography and OCT to detect even the earliest changes.

🔵

Glaucoma Screening

Known as the "silent thief of sight," glaucoma damages the optic nerve — usually without pain or vision change — until significant irreversible loss has occurred. Anyone over 40, or with a family history of glaucoma, should have their intraocular pressure and optic nerve assessed yearly. Our Goldmann applanation tonometry and OCT nerve fibre analysis catch it early.

  3. Preliminary Testing — A trained ophthalmic technician performs initial tests: visual acuity (the chart test), auto-refraction (your current glasses power), intraocular pressure (glaucoma screening), and colour vision if indicated. This typically takes 15–20 minutes.
  4. Doctor Consultation — The surgeon reviews your test results, examines your eyes with a slit lamp, and — if required — dilates your pupils for a detailed fundus (retina) examination. The doctor explains every finding in clear language and answers all your questions.
  5. Treatment Plan & Next Steps — Whether it's a prescription update, a pre-LASIK workup, or a referral for surgery, you'll leave with a clear written plan, a summary of your diagnoses, and a direct contact for any follow-up questions.

Do I need to fast or prepare anything before an eye check-up?

No fasting is required for a standard eye examination. However, if you wear contact lenses, we recommend removing them at least 3 days before a LASIK evaluation and at least 24 hours before a routine checkup (soft lenses) to allow your cornea to return to its natural shape. Please carry your current glasses prescription if you have one.

Can I get LASIK consultation on my first visit?

Yes. If you would like to know whether you are a LASIK candidate, mention it when booking — we'll schedule the pre-LASIK workup (corneal topography, pachymetry, dilated examination) in the same visit. Many patients from Goregaon East get the full evaluation and a clear answer on suitability in a single 90-minute appointment.

Does Sentra Clinic have Saturday appointments for working professionals?

Yes. Saturday OPD is available. We specifically offer early morning (8:30 AM) and Saturday slots to accommodate working professionals from Goregaon East, NESCO, and Filmcity who cannot take weekday leave. Mention your preference when booking.

I'm diabetic — how often should I get my eyes checked?

Every diabetic should have a dilated fundus examination at least once a year, even if their vision seems perfectly normal. Diabetic retinopathy causes no symptoms in its early, treatable stage. If your sugar control is poor or you have had diabetes for over 10 years, we may recommend a check-up every 6 months. Call us to schedule.

What are the signs that I need to see an eye specialist urgently?

Seek urgent eye care for: sudden loss or blurring of vision in one or both eyes, flashes of light or a sudden shower of new floaters, a shadow or curtain across part of your vision, severe eye pain with redness, or any chemical or physical injury to the eye. Call 93729 47075 directly for emergency guidance.
