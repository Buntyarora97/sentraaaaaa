Frequently Asked Questions — Andheri to Sentra Clinic

How far is Sentra Clinic from Andheri?

By road, Sentra Clinic is approximately 20–25 minutes from Andheri via the Western Express Highway or S.V. Road. By Western Railway train, Andheri to Malad is just 4 stations (about 12 minutes on a fast train), plus 10 minutes by auto-rickshaw to Rani Sati Road.

Is there a good eye hospital in Andheri itself?

Andheri has many optometrists and spectacle shops, and some general hospitals with eye OPDs. However, for dedicated surgical eye care — LASIK, cataract with premium IOLs, retinal procedures, or glaucoma surgery — Sentra Clinic in Malad East is the nearest full-service surgical eye hospital with resident fellowship-trained surgeons and an in-house OT.

Walk-ins from Andheri are welcome, but we strongly recommend booking ahead by phone (93729 47075) or WhatsApp to guarantee your slot, especially before making the journey. Appointments ensure you are seen by the surgeon without waiting.

Dr. Modi's OPD: Monday 11am–1pm & 6–8:30pm; Tuesday 1–5pm & 7:30–9pm; Wednesday & Thursday 4:30–8:30pm; Friday 3–8pm; Saturday 1:30–8:30pm; Sunday closed. Please WhatsApp for Dr. Shraddha's schedule.

Can I get my LASIK or cataract consultation and surgery done in one trip?

No — a consultation visit is always separate from surgery. The consultation includes pupil dilation, which temporarily affects your vision for 4–6 hours. We will discuss findings and schedule surgery on a subsequent visit. This is for your safety and the quality of your outcome.
