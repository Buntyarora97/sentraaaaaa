Cataract Surgery for Seniors

Many Goregaon West seniors in housing societies have been told they "need cataract surgery but it's too early." LASIK for Young Professionals

Young Goregaon West adults — many working in the Mindspace offices or commuting to BKC and Andheri — are a large portion of our LASIK consultations. The desire to be free from glasses or contact lenses is very strong in this age group. Contoura Vision (topography-guided LASIK) is our most popular procedure for this demographic, offering sharper results than standard LASIK by mapping each eye's unique corneal topography. Average procedure time: 10–12 minutes per eye.

💧

Dry Eye & Computer Vision Syndrome

With most Goregaon West working adults spending 8–10 hours at screens daily, dry eye disease and computer vision syndrome are endemic. Symptoms — stinging, burning, fluctuating vision, and end-of-day eye fatigue — are often dismissed as "just tiredness." Our dry eye clinic performs a complete evaluation including blink dynamics, tear film stability, and meibomian gland function to identify the specific type of dry eye and prescribe targeted therapy.

🩺

Diabetic Retinopathy & Hypertensive Retinopathy

Among the middle-aged and senior population of Goregaon West societies, diabetes and hypertension are extremely common — and both conditions affect the retina. Diabetic retinopathy can lead to permanent blindness if untreated, yet it is completely asymptomatic in its early stages. We perform annual dilated retina exams, fundus photography, and OCT imaging to detect and treat changes at the earliest, most manageable stage.

🔵

Glaucoma

Goregaon West has a large population of adults over 40 who have never had their intraocular pressure (IOP) checked. Glaucoma damages peripheral vision first — and because it progresses very slowly, patients rarely notice until significant loss has occurred. A simple annual IOP check, combined with our OCT optic nerve analysis, can diagnose glaucoma years before it becomes visually symptomatic.

👶

Children's Eye Problems

School-going children in Goregaon West frequently present with increasing spectacle power (myopia), squint, and occasional amblyopia (lazy eye). Myopia progression in children aged 7–14 is now being treated proactively — through orthokeratology, atropine therapy, or specialised spectacle lenses — to slow down the rate of increase, reducing adult myopia risk. Early intervention saves children from high power prescriptions and the associated risks.

Can I get cataract surgery done the same week as consultation?

In many cases, yes. If your pre-operative evaluation confirms you are ready for surgery and the OT schedule permits, surgery can be scheduled within the same week. However, certain conditions (uncontrolled blood sugar, elevated BP) may require medical optimisation first. Your doctor will advise after the evaluation.

Is LASIK suitable for me if I have a high prescription?

Standard LASIK corrects up to approximately −10 diopters depending on corneal thickness. For very high myopia (beyond −8 to −10), or if corneas are found to be thin on topography, ICL surgery (Implantable Collamer Lens) is the better option — and Sentra Clinic offers this as well. A proper pre-LASIK workup will give you a clear answer.

Do you screen for glaucoma during a routine checkup?

Yes. All comprehensive eye checkups at Sentra Clinic include intraocular pressure (IOP) measurement and a doctor's assessment of the optic disc. For patients with elevated IOP or a family history of glaucoma, we perform additional OCT nerve fibre layer analysis and visual field testing.

My child's power keeps increasing every year — what should I do?

Progressive myopia in children is now treated, not just monitored. Options include low-dose atropine eye drops, myopia-control spectacle lenses, and orthokeratology. The earlier treatment starts, the more effective it is. Book a paediatric evaluation for your child at Sentra Clinic.
